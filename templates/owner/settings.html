How to connect the board

  1. Plug in the board and wait about a minute.
  2. On your phone, join the WiFi network {{ setup_ssid }} (password: {{ setup_password }}).
  3. A setup page opens by itself. If it does not, open http://192.168.4.1/ in your browser.
  4. Choose your home WiFi, type its password, and enter your setup code. Leave the server address as it is.
  5. Press Save. The board restarts and joins your WiFi; the setup network disappears.

Keep this open. Within a minute or two the status above should say Connected.

What to check

  1. Is the board plugged in and its light on? After a power cut it reconnects by itself within a minute.
  2. Is your home WiFi working, with internet? Try opening any website on your phone.
  3. Did the WiFi name or password change? The board needs to be told: unplug it, wait two minutes, plug it in - the {{ setup_ssid }} network reappears and you can enter the new WiFi, without a code.
  4. Still nothing after that? Contact the water system facilitator.

How to install on an iPhone or iPad
  1. Open this page in Safari. Chrome on an iPhone cannot install it.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen — the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add at the top right.

The icon appears on your home screen with your other apps.

How to install on an Android phone
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app, or Add to Home screen.
  4. Tap Install.
