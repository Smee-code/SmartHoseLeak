Two kinds of people arrive: a household or the facilitator on their way to signing in, and - the one this redesign is for - a defence panel deciding whether this is a real, engineered system. So the page is the face of a chart recorder. The hero is not a chart dropped onto a landing page; it is the recorder itself, drawing the one pattern the whole system turns on: pressure sliding away while no tap is open. Everything is drawn in-page - inline SVG, one self-hosted font - because the rest of the app is an offline PWA on a weak signal and this page keeps that discipline. The trace is an illustration of the pattern, captioned as such, never dressed up as a live reading: on the one page where a stranger decides whether to trust the rest, that honesty is the whole point. {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
