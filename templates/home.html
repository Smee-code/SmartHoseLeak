How the system works

Four stages, from the sensor in the field to the notice on a household phone.

●

1. Sense

A flow sensor and a pressure sensor on each hose line feed an ESP32, which samples every second and filters the readings.

⚠

2. Detect

Flow that runs higher than the calibrated pressure–flow curve, or pressure that decays while no tap is open, is flagged as a possible leak.

⚑

3. Locate

Distance markers along each line narrow the fault to the segment it sits in, so the facilitator knows which stretch to walk.

✉

4. Alert

An SMS goes to the facilitator immediately, and every household on the affected line gets a notice in the Owner Portal.
